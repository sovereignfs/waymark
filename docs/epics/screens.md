places, Home, and the phone. Every map has a list beside or instead of it.

#### ✅ 7.2 — Checking in

**Goal:** the check-in sheet. **Design:** §9.2, §20.5. **Depends on:** 7.1, 4.2, 17.1.

**Technical:** `Sheet` wide, `Drawer` narrow, the same content; _Here_ (position asked for when first used, the person's nearest places, then the place
search's nearby, then _New place here_), _Search_, _On the map_ (17.1's point picking); the moment (now, or earlier, with the zone following the place);
note; photos (camera or files, through 6.5); companions (`TagInput`); offline, own places only, a new place by name and position, or a point alone; Enter
to check in; denied location removes _Here_ with a sentence. The trip link and sharing rows appear with 9.7 and 10.3.

**Accepts when:** in a browser with position mocked, each way checks in; with the network off, a check-in by position alone is saved and later named by
reverse lookup; the sheet works with the keyboard only.

**Done as, except _On the map_, which waits for `Map` (17.1, in `sovereign`) and is not offered until it exists:** **`src/screens/checkin-flow.ts`**, pure (every way and every odd turn tested without a browser): `buildOptions` offers **the person's own places first** (searched on the device, nearest first when the position is known, those within 1.5 km when nothing is typed), then the place search's with any the person already has taken out, then _New place here_ and _A place near …_ only where there is a position; `selectionOf`, `zoneOfSelection` and `momentFromInput` (the moment on the **place's** clock,
23:59 and 00:01 on the right days); and `planCheckIn`, which makes the action, **reusing a place the person has however it was reached** (the same source and id, or the same name within 50 metres, so "Café de Flore" by search and by name at the spot is one place and two check-ins). **The sheet's content (`CheckInContent`)**: _Here_ (the position is **asked for when it is first used and never needed**; if the browser already allows it, it is read without a question and _Here_ is first; if it is refused or the device cannot tell, _Here_ goes with a sentence and the
person is left on _Search_), _Search_ (the field has the focus when the sheet opens; own places at once, the place search after a 450 ms pause and for three characters or more, **the position never sent with a search**), a new place by name at the position, a point alone, the moment (now, or **Another time** on the clock where the place is, in its zone, **with the sentence for a moment in the future given by the app and not by the browser**: the form does not use the browser's own validation), a note, photos (files or the camera, up to ten, shown and removable
before they are kept, each kept on the device under an id the check-in names and uploaded as 6.5 does), companions (`TagInput`), and **Check in**, which **saves and closes at once**: the check-in is on the device and goes when there is a network. **Enter checks in** from the search field or a chosen place, taking the first place on offer when none is chosen. A place search that is **off**, **not reachable** or **needs a connection** says so in a sentence and the person's own places are still there. The browser tests' main instance now
searches the fake place search (`src/places/fake.ts`, started by Playwright), so the search is tried for real and reaches nothing. **Three real faults found by the browser tests, and fixed:** (1) the sheet opened and closed by the **router**, which asks the server for the page again, so **with no network it fell back to loading the page and failed**; the address is now read from `window.location` and changed by the **history API** (`checkin-location.ts`), which asks for nothing, **and the sheet is told at once** (the browser's back button too), so it opens and closes immediately instead of waiting for Next to take
the new address up, which a busy page did late enough to fail a WebKit test now and then (Next keeps `usePathname` and `useSearchParams` in step, and the sheet still closes when the app navigates elsewhere); (2) **`store.view()` read the rows and the outbox in two transactions**, so a sync settling between them left a check-in, or the place it was made at, out of what was shown for a moment, which made a search typed at that moment miss the person's own place (an intermittent failure, one run in about five); it reads in one transaction now, and a test fixes that;
(3) a click on the avatar menu before a cold server's page had started up did nothing, so the sign-out helper asks again if the menu has not opened. **Seen, in Chromium and WebKit (`e2e/checkin.spec.ts`):** with the position mocked, _Here_ is first, a new place by name makes a place in its zone and a check-in, and a second check-in by search is **the same place** (one place, two check-ins); at Tokyo Tower the search's place is offered and makes a place with its source, id, category and zone; search finds Café Central and Enter checks in at the first when none is chosen; **with the network off, a check-in by
position alone is saved on the device** (the sheet opens, works and closes with no network) **and goes when the network is back**, the place called "A place near …" in the zone of its point (the server names it from where it is when its job runs, 5.2); with the network off the person's own places are still searched and the sheet says the place search needs a connection; another time is on the place's clock (23:59 of a day in the past, in Tokyo, whatever the device's zone) and tomorrow is refused with the sentence; a refused position removes _Here_ with its sentence and searching
still works with no way to make a place; a note, two companions and photos (one removed before saving) are kept with the check-in and the photo reaches the server; the keyboard alone (`C`, type, Enter) checks in; and at 375 px it is the drawer and works the same. The whole browser suite passed eight times in a row. **Left out:** the sheet filled in from Trip Mode or an item.

**Finished later (_On the map_, the trip link and sharing):** **_On the map_** is the sheet's third way (`PointPicker`, the design system's `Map` as a place to choose a point, from `@sovereignfs/ui/map`, loaded only where it is drawn): a click or tap chooses the point and, with the map focused, the arrow keys move it and Enter chooses its middle; the point is then the position the sheet offers places near, as _Here_'s is (the person's own places, the place search's by reverse lookup, _New place here…_ and a point alone). It is offered only where the server has a map (`useMapStyle`) and not when changing a check-in. **The device's auto-link hook** (`src/autolink/device.ts`, 6 tests): the trips the device holds as candidates (a Traveler, or an Organizer who is going; a trip with no dates, and a stop with none, are none) and the hook an action asks (`decide`, the same pure function the server runs), so **a check-in falls into its trip at once, with no network**; the server decides again when it applies the change. **Seen (`e2e/checkin.spec.ts`):** on the map, a click in the middle of the map chooses the person's position, a new place is named there and the check-in is made at the point (to within what a click gives). **The photo viewer** is on the check-in's page (`PhotoGalleryOf`, the design system's gallery): thumbnails opening a full-screen viewer, Escape closing it and the focus coming back to the thumbnail, a photo from the device while it is only there and from `/api/media` otherwise (tested in the same spec). **Trip and sharing** (`CheckinTrip`, on the check-in's page, e2e in `e2e/checkin-trip.spec.ts`): the trip it is part of with the trip's dot and name and _Suggested_ or _Linked by you_, _Change the trip_ / _Link to a trip_, _Unlink_ and _Let Waymark suggest_ (hands a manual link back to the app) , the check-in's own word about sharing (_Follow what I chose for the trip_, _Share with the trip_, _Keep private_) and a mark when it is shared; and **the first time a check-in falls into a trip with other people and the person has not said, it asks once** (_Share your check-ins on … with its N members?_, which is the member's choice for the trip, `member.share`). All are changes recorded on the device, which work with no network. **Left out:** the question's answer is not tested in a browser (it needs a trip with a second person, which the invitation instance has and this spec does not use); the question does not know the person's default in Settings (it is asked when their choice for the trip is still _ask_, as the trip's page shows it); a mark for "shared" on a row of the timeline.

#### ✅ 7.4 — The map

**Goal:** `/map`. **Design:** §8.2, §20.7. **Depends on:** 7.3, 4.3, 17.1.

**Technical:** every check-in clustered from the store; the timeline's filters; a selected marker shows the place, times and last visit; the list of what
is in view beside the map wide, and alone when `WAYMARK_MAP_SOURCE` is `off`; the style follows the theme.

**Accepts when:** the map shows a seeded decade without request off the origin, and the list matches the markers in view.

**Done as, except the list "in view" (it is every place that matches):** **`/map` is the person's map** (`MapScreen`, which the server's page and the offline shell both draw): **every place the filtered check-ins are at, each once as a marker that says how often** ("Café de Flore, 3 visits"), clustered by the design system's `Map` where they are close, from the device's own store. **A place is a marker and not a check-in**, so a decade of ten thousand check-ins is a few hundred markers (`src/screens/mapview.ts`, pure, 5 tests, built in well under a second); **the list and the markers are made from the same rows** (most visited first, then the latest visit), and **the timeline's filters are here, in the same query** (`FilterBar`, now shared with the timeline: search, year, category, country and trip), so a filter means the same on both screens and in a link, and the visits are the filtered check-ins' only. **A marker, or its row, chooses the place**: its name, its kind and city, "You've been here 3 times", its first and last visit and **Open the place** and **Last check-in**. **The list is always there**, beside the map on a wide screen and under it on a narrow one, **alone with a sentence** where the server has no map (`WAYMARK_MAP_SOURCE=off`) or none can be reached; with no network the list is what is there (the browser may still hold the style; the tiles are the server's and are not). It asks no address but this server's (the worker is served from it too, 11.4); the style follows the theme (`useMapStyle`). **Seen:** the unit tests (the filters taken out made two fail); **in Chromium (`e2e/map.spec.ts`):** three places in three countries are three markers and three rows, Lisbon first with "3 visits", the marker labels say the place and how often, choosing a row shows the visits and the links to the place and its last check-in, a country filter leaves one marker and one row and is in the address, clearing it and a year leaves the visits of that year, a reload keeps the filter, **and no request went to any origin but this server's**; and the offline spec now finds the person's places in the list with no network. **Left out:** **the list of "what is in view"**: the design system's `Map` has no callback for the view it is showing, so the list is every place that matches, and the map's clustering and zoom do the narrowing by eye (raise in `sovereign`: an `onMove` with the bounds); the list is the first 300 places and says how many more; a marker for each check-in rather than each place; a seeded decade in a browser (the unit test builds ten thousand check-ins in under a second; `pnpm seed` makes a decade for development); WebKit was not run for this task.

#### ✅ 7.5 — Places and a place

**Goal:** `/places` and `/places/<id>`. **Design:** §20.8. **Depends on:** 7.3.

**Technical:** places by visits with a search; a place's page with its small map, "You've been here N times", first and last, its check-ins; rename,
recategorize, move the point, merge, delete with none (4.1's operations, online).

**Accepts when:** merging two places moves their check-ins and the count adds up.

**Done as, except moving a place's point and the small map, which wait for `Map` (17.1, in `sovereign`), and the trips a place was part of (9.7):** **`/places` and `/places/<id>` are one page** (`PlacesView`) **that draws what the address says**, as the timeline does, so opening a place, searching and coming back change the address by the history API and ask the server for nothing: they work with no network, and a reload on `/places/<id>` is the same page (`CheckinsLayout`, still the only file that asks the width, is given the panes' names). **`src/screens/placelist.ts`**, pure and tested: every place with its visits, first and last (`placeStats`), **most visited first, then the latest visit, then name, then id**, so the order is the same on every device whatever order the rows come in; the search (`filterStats`, over `searchPlaces`: every word, any order, no case or accents; punctuation is no word, so a pattern is read as words and runs nothing); the check-ins at a place, newest first; "You've been here 4 times"; and the **window** (`windowRows`: rows of a fixed height, a few dozen drawn at any scroll position of ten thousand). **The list** (`PlacesList`) keeps the search in the address (`?q=`) and carries it to a place and back. **A place** (`PlaceDetail`): name, category, address, the sentence, first and last visit on the clock where each was made, its check-ins as links, **Edit** (name and category; the place sent whole with the version it was made from, `updatePlace`, and "Nothing was changed." for nothing), **Merge into…** (a `Combobox` of the person's other places) and **Delete** (disabled with a sentence while any check-in is there, asked about first, for good). **Rename, recategorize and delete were already actions of 5.1; merging is new, and it is not an online operation: it is `mergePlaces`** (`actions/places.ts`), which puts each check-in at the place again, whole and with its version, and deletes the first place, **through the one write path**, so it works offline, is applied by the server as any other change (both places and every check-in re-checked as the person's, in one short transaction that numbers each change in the stream), and reaches the person's other devices through the stream with the place as a tombstone. A place with more check-ins than a mutation holds (50) is moved over several mutations **in order, the delete last and with the final batch**: the outbox sends them in order, and a delete that came before a move is refused as `in_use` and goes to Couldn't sync with its sentence, nothing lost. A check-in's moment, zone and everything but the place stay as they were. **A check-in's page now links its place's name to the place** (left out by 7.3). **Not `mergePlace`** of `places/places.ts` (4.1), which moves rows with no number in the stream and so would never reach a device: it stays for what needs it (a script, a test) and nothing in the app calls it. **Seen:** the actions (one check-in, none, 120 over three mutations of 49, 49 and 23, the same place and a missing one refused), the server applying a merge of 60 check-ins over two mutations (all at the new place, the count the same, a new version for each, a tombstone, a replay writing nothing), the last mutation alone refused as `in_use` with the place kept, and a second person's attempt changing nothing; **in Chromium (`e2e/places.spec.ts`):** places by visits with their counts and the unused last, a search by name, category and address and the empty state, a place beside the list at `/places/<id>?q=…` with the sentence and dates, a reload keeping it, a check-in of the place linking back to it, and at 375 px a page with "← Places"; a place that is not here says so; **renaming with the network off** (nothing changed said, then name and category changed at once, and the server has them when the network is back); **merging with the network off** (the page becomes the other place with three visits, the first leaves the list, the server drops it when the network is back, and a reload agrees); **deleting a place with no check-in with the network off** (asked first, kept on "Keep it"). Each safeguard of the merge was taken out in turn and seen to fail its test (the room for the delete, its being last). The whole Chromium suite passed (68); **WebKit was not run** (its browser is not installed on this machine), and the data-layer legs on sqld and PostgreSQL were not run (Docker); the change adds no table or statement. **Left out:** moving a place's point and the small map of a place (17.1), the trips a place was part of (9.7), and a place's own export (15.1).
