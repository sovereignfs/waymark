# Epic 10: Sharing

## Status

📋 Planned.

## Overview

Trips with the people going and the people following: three roles by how one takes part (Organizer, Traveler, Follower), invitations by notice, email or
link, check-ins private unless shared with the trip, and leaving without losing anything. Invitations follow Tally's (its §6.3); there are no
placeholders here.

[`DESIGN.md`](../../DESIGN.md) §5.2, §6, §11, §23

## Tasks

#### ✅ 10.1 — Roles

**Goal:** what each role may do, in every statement. **Design:** §11.1, §5.2. **Depends on:** 9.1.

**Technical:** `trip_member.role` and `going`; the role sets per operation and mutation in `src/access/`, used by `/api/ops`, sync (6.6) and media (9.5);
change a role, a person's "going"; the last Organizer held; `role_changed` raised once 12.1 is in; the test calling every trip operation as each lower role.

**Accepts when:** a Traveler cannot invite or delete; a Follower cannot edit anything or see an attachment; a trip never has no Organizer.

**Done as:** **The role sets** are in `src/access/trip.ts`, one place for every statement: `ORGANIZERS`, `EDITORS` (Organizers and Travelers) and `MEMBERS`, and now **`ATTACHMENT_READERS` (= `EDITORS`: never Followers)**, which 9.5's statements and 6.6's pull must use; `OPERATIONS` in `src/trips/ops.ts` says which set each operation needs, and the test that calls every operation as a stranger and as each lower role is driven by it (it fails to compile for an operation with no request, and now covers 21). **`src/trips/members.ts`:** **`setRole`** (`member.role`: an Organizer makes another member, or themselves, an Organizer, a Traveler or a Follower; **who goes follows the role** (Organizers and Travelers go, a Follower does not) and asking for the role they have changes and says nothing, advancing no counter) and **`setGoing`** (`member.going`: an Organizer's own "Not going" switch; a Traveler goes and a Follower does not, so they have no switch, and it is only ever one's own). **The last Organizer is held in the statement that changes the role** (`role <> 'organizer' OR another active Organizer exists`), after a guarded `UPDATE trip` that shows the caller is an Organizer and takes the trip's row, so that two Organizers demoting themselves, or each other, at the same moment leave one (tested both ways; with the clause taken out the sequential test and the concurrent self-demotion test fail); the refusal says so in a sentence. **A person who is not in the trip or has left it, a Traveler, a Follower and a stranger are all told it is not there**, the same words. **`role_changed`** (a new kind: the bell and core's bell by default) tells the person whose role changed, in a sentence that names the trip and the role, never the Organizer who changed it, and nothing when they changed their own; what it says is sealed. A role change takes effect at once for every statement (a Follower made a Traveler edits the plan, and one made a Follower stops; tested). **Seen**, on a `file:` database, sqld and PostgreSQL (the whole suite, 1345 tests, on each): every role and who goes, the counter, the last Organizer, concurrency, who may and who is told it is not there, the notice, the switch and the reset by a change of role. **Left out:** nothing adds a person to a trip yet (invitations, 10.2) or lets one leave or be removed (10.4, which hold the same last-Organizer rule), so a trip still has the people a test puts in it; "Ask an Organizer to invite" is a screen (11.7); sync's role checks per mutation are 6.6 and media's are 9.5, both using the sets above; and a Traveler lowered to Follower between syncs, whose item edit goes to Couldn't sync, is 6.6's.

#### 📋 10.2 — Invitations

**Goal:** bringing people into a trip with a role. **Design:** §11.2, §6.2. **Depends on:** 10.1, 12.2, 17.2.

**Technical:** `invitation` (one trip, one role; email sealed); by a name of someone the Organizer shares a trip with (a notice), by email (a notice to
an account with it, mail through 12.2 to any other address; the lookup rate-limited per account and hour), or as a link; token of 32 random bytes, stored
as its hash, compared in constant time, 14 days, single-use, revocable, resent once an hour; `/invite/<token>` showing the trip's name, dates and inviter
only, every invalid link the same page; accept by signing in or signing up (`WAYMARK_AUTH_SIGNUP=invite` from 17.2, the default from then); `invited` and
`invite_accepted`; the manifest's scopes gain `offline_access` and `directory` (used by 14.2).

**Accepts when:** a link sent through any channel is accepted by whoever holds it, once; an expired, revoked or used link shows the same page as a
wrong one; with no mail set up, the dialog offers the link and says email is not set up.

#### ✅ 10.3 — Sharing check-ins with a trip

**Goal:** check-ins private by default, shared by choice. **Design:** §11.3, §15.3. **Depends on:** 10.1, 6.6, 9.7.

**Technical:** `checkin.share` (`trip`, `on`, `off`) and `trip_member.share_checkins` (`ask`, `on`, `off`); the once-per-trip question on the first linked
check-in; `shared_checkin`, the reader's sealed copy in the trip's stream, written when shared or changed and removed when unshared, unlinked or the author
leaves; photos of a shared check-in served to the trip's members through 3.2's check; only Travelers' check-ins link and share; Settings > Check-ins'
default; `checkin_shared` (off by default) once 12.1 is in.

**Accepts when:** an unshared linked check-in never reaches another member's device or the server's answers to them; sharing makes it appear for a

**Done as:** **The rule is pure** (`src/sharing/rules.ts`, no database, for the device to run when trips are on it, 6.6): `sharedWith` says which trip a check-in is shared with, if any. It needs the check-in linked to a trip, its author **still an Organizer or a Traveler** of it, and then the check-in's own word (`on` shares, `off` keeps private whatever the trip says) or, when it says `trip`, the author's **choice for the trip** (`trip_member.share_checkins`), which while it is still `ask` is answered by their **default in Settings** (`profile.share_default`); **nothing is shared until the person has said so**, so the default of everything is private. `needsAnswer` is the once-per-trip question (a check-in that follows the trip, in a trip with other members, with nothing answering it), for the screen. **The copy** is the new table `shared_checkin` (migration `0022_sharing`, both dialects, in the table list and in rotation's phases): the check-in id (its key, so a check-in has at most one), the trip, the author, the **trip's number at the change** (`seq`) and a **sealed** body (`SEALED.shared_checkin`): the place (name, point, address, category), moment, zone, offset, note, companions and the **ids** of its photos, and nothing else of the author. **`src/sharing/copies.ts` `syncCopy` is the one function that makes a copy what it should be**, in the transaction of every change that could alter it: the check-in being written (`src/sync/apply.ts`, after its photos) or deleted, **its place being edited** (so a reader never holds the old name or point), a **job re-linking it** (`src/autolink/queue.ts`), the author's **role changing** (`setRole`: a Traveler made a Follower has nothing shared any more, made a Traveler again it comes back) and their **choice for the trip changing**. It writes, replaces, **moves to another trip** (the old trip is told of the removal) or removes the copy, each taking the trip's next number; **the statement that takes the number names the author's membership as Organizer or Traveler**, so a copy is never written for someone who may not share. **`setShareChoice`** (`member.share`, an online operation for Organizers and Travelers; for a Follower, a former member, a stranger and a trip that is not there, the same "not found") records the answer and makes the author's check-ins in that trip right in the same transaction: `on` shares the ones that follow the trip, `off` takes them back, and one that says `on` or `off` for itself is not touched. **What members are given** is `listShared` (`checkin.shared`, every member, Followers too): the **copies, never the originals**, opened on the server for the reader, past a number (`after`), the author's own left out; **an unshared check-in has no row, so there is nothing of it in any answer to another member**. **Photos:** `/api/media/<id>` (`readFileFor`) serves a photo, **and its thumbnail**, to a member of a trip the check-in is shared with (one statement joins the photo to its copy and to the reader's membership), after their own files and the attachments they may see; a stranger, a former member and a member of another trip are not given it, and unsharing stops it at once. **Leaving and deleting:** deleting a trip removes its copies (`shared_checkin` is `delete` in `TRIP_TABLES`, the originals stay with their authors, unlinked); deleting an account removes theirs from every trip, **before** any trip they were alone in goes (`endMembershipsOf`, tested with one of each in `people.test.ts`); `dropCopiesOf` is what 10.4's leave and remove call. **`checkin_shared`** (a new notice kind, off in every channel by default): raised in the transaction, once when a check-in is first shared (not when it is edited), **only for the members who turned it on**, naming the trip and nothing of the check-in or its author. **Seen**, on a `file:` database, sqld and PostgreSQL: the whole suite (1480 tests; 24 of them new) and the pure rule (6); private by default and after "no"; given to every member with its place, note, companions and photos and nothing of the author; a stranger, a former member and a trip that is not there are told alike; unsharing, unlinking by hand, linking to another trip and deleting each remove it and take a number; an edit of the check-in and of its place reach the copy; `after` returns only what changed; another account cannot touch a copy; the trip's choice and Settings' default; who may choose; a role change; the job re-linking after the trip is re-dated; leaving; deleting the trip; the photos and thumbnails to Followers and not to strangers; the notice to those who chose it only. With each of these taken out one test fails: the author's role in the rule, a choice of `ask` meaning `on`, the membership in the photo's statement, the copy's removal on delete, on a role change, on a place edit and in the job. **Two guards are defence in depth and no test can reach them alone** (taking either out changes nothing seen, since the rule and the reader's first statement already refuse): the membership in the statement that takes the trip's number, and in the reader's list query. **Left out:** **the trip's stream on devices** is 6.6, done since: it pulls these copies past a trip's cursor, with a tombstone when a copy is removed (`dropCopy` and `dropCopiesOf` record it); **the per-trip choice syncing offline** (§15.2) waits for an action on the device, and is online (`member.share`) until then; **the screens** (the question on the check-in, "Share with the trip / Keep private", the trip page's choice, Settings > Check-ins) are 7.x and 11.x, over the operations that exist; the notice is not raised for a check-in a re-linking job shares, only for one a person's sync does, and **a bell-only choice for `checkin_shared`** needs a preference for the bell itself (a member who turns on email or core's bell is told in the bell too); the lock order between a check-in being applied and a trip being changed, which 10.4 settled (`access/locks.ts`); and **export and import (15.3)** do not carry copies, which are other people's views of a check-in and are made again when the original is.
Follower at the next sync, and unsharing removes it.

#### ✅ 10.4 — Leaving, removing and handing over

**Goal:** people leave without losing anything. **Design:** §11.4, §23. **Depends on:** 10.1, 10.3.

**Technical:** leave (anyone; the last Organizer must name another), remove (Organizers); both unlink and unshare the person's check-ins from the trip in
the same transaction and end their membership; `People.deletePerson` gains the trip rules: hand over to the longest-standing Traveler, then Follower
(`organizer_passed`), delete a trip with no one left, clear `created_by` on items, attachments and links.

**Accepts when:** after leaving, nothing of the person's check-ins is in the trip's stream; deleting the only Organizer's account hands the trip over and

**Done as:** **`src/trips/leaving.ts`**: `leaveTrip` (`member.leave`, any member) and `removeMember` (`member.remove`, Organizers only), each **one transaction** with the same effect on the person: their check-ins are **unlinked from the trip in their own stream** (`unlinkCheckins`, now for one person: a new version and number each so their devices find out; the check-ins stay in their log untouched), their **copies removed from the trip's stream** (`dropCopiesOf`, with a tombstone, so members' devices drop them), and their membership **ended** (`left_at`; the row stays so the trip can still say who was there; what they planned and attached stays, as the trip's). The permission is in the statement: a guarded `UPDATE trip` shows the caller is a member (leaving) or an Organizer (removing) and takes the trip's number; a Follower leaves too; a stranger, a former member, a trip that is not there and a person who is not in the trip are all told it is not there, and "remove yourself" is said only to an Organizer ("To leave a trip, use Leave"). **The last Organizer** of a trip with other people in it must name `successor`, an active member of any part, who **becomes an Organizer in the same transaction** and is told (`role_changed`); with nobody else in the trip, leaving is refused in words and deleting is the way; when another Organizer remains, no name is asked and one given changes nobody. The trip never has no Organizer: the words and the guarded statement keep it, and the statement that ends the membership repeats the rule (`role <> 'organizer' OR another active Organizer exists`). **A removed person is told in the bell** ("You were removed from “Iberia”", `role_changed`, which §17.1 already says covers removal) in words that name the trip and no one, as a notice about no trip (a notice about a trip the person is not in is not shown, so it carries none). **The lock order** that 10.3 left open is settled in `src/access/locks.ts`: the account's stream is taken before a trip's row everywhere; leaving, removing, deleting a trip and an account being deleted now take the streams they are about to write first (`lockStreams`, a statement that changes nothing), so on PostgreSQL two that meet cannot wait for each other. **Account deletion's trip rules** (hand over to the longest-standing Traveler, then Follower, and tell them; delete a trip with no one left; clear `created_by`) were done with 9.1 in `endMembershipsOf`; here they also remove the person's copies first (10.3) and tell the other members' devices that the member row is gone (6.6). **Seen**, on a `file:` database, sqld and PostgreSQL (the whole suite, 1539 tests; 19 of them new): nothing of a leaver's check-ins is in the trip's stream, their devices are told, the others' are untouched, the members' devices are told of the copy's removal and of the member, the leaver is told the trip is gone, and a re-link does not link them again; their photos are no longer served to the trip, and what they planned stays; every refusal; the hand-over, its notice, the alone case, the second Organizer, and two Organizers leaving at once (one stands); removal with the same effect and the notice, by Organizers only; the lock helper changes no counter. With each of these taken out one test fails: the unlink, the copies, only-this-person's unlink, the Organizer check on removal, and the hand-over rule. **One guard is defence in depth**: the repeat of the Organizer rule in the statement that ends the membership (the trip's row is already held and the words refuse first). **Left out:** the screens (the People view's Leave and Remove, and the hand-over choice: 11.7); an invitation back is 10.2; and a notice to the other members that someone left (the design does not ask for one).
tells the new Organizer; a trip with no one left is gone.
