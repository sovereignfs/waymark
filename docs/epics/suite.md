# Epic 14: The suite

## Status

✅ Done.

## Overview

What a Sovereign instance adds when it hosts Waymark: core's bell, email and activity page, and its directory to find people to invite. Each is an addition: a
call that fails, is refused or is not supported changes nothing but that call, and every feature works the same without it.

## Related

[`DESIGN.md`](../../DESIGN.md) §17.2, §19.3, §19.4, §27

## Tasks

#### ✅ 14.1 — Core's bell, email, activity

**Goal:** core's services for people linked to core. **Design:** §17.2, §19.4. **Depends on:** 2.3, 12.3.

**Technical:** through `@sovereignfs/sdk` on the server: `sv.app.notifications.send` for notices where core's bell is on (a link back to the notice's
place), `sv.app.email.sendToUser` for linked accounts in place of Waymark's mail where granted, `sv.app.activity.log` for export, import and deletion; each
with a `key`; `NotSupportedError`, a refusal or no core changes nothing else; Settings shows core's bell as a channel only when hosted.

**Accepts when:** against `createFakeCore`, each call is made once with its key; with the scopes not granted or an older core, the bell and Waymark's mail
still work.

**Done as:** **`src/sovereign/services.ts`**: the SDK's `sv.app` (the app as itself: it asks core for a token for the app and holds no person's token, so it needs neither a token store nor a session store) behind one guarded client, `notify`, `email` and `activity`, **none of which throws**: each answers `sent`, `no_channel` (no sub, or an older core that does not serve it: `NotSupportedError`), `not_permitted` (403, the scope not granted), `retry_after` (429, with core's pause) or `failed` (down, timed out, a lost answer). A core that refuses or does not serve a service is logged once and **rested for six hours**, so a service that cannot work is not asked again and fills no log; text is cut to core's limits; nothing is logged but the service and what became of it. Every call carries a `key`. **`core_outbox`** (migration `0015_core_outbox`; its content sealed, `SEALED.core_outbox`, in the design's table list and in the key rotation's phases): a call is **queued in the transaction of the change that causes it** and made by the `send-core` job, so nothing is held across a call to core. It is the mail outbox's shape: a lease so that two workers or processes make one call once, the mail outbox's growing pauses, a pause core asked for taken as asked, given up on after six tries or a day. **Which calls:** `raiseNotice` queues **core's bell** (`sv.app.notifications.send`, with a link back to the notice's page) where the person wants it, and **core's email** (`sv.app.email.sendToUser`, in the core's own template, to the person named by their `sub`, never given their address) in place of Waymark's mail for a person who signs in through the core; **an email core will not send (not granted, an old core, or a person with no `sub` there) falls back to Waymark's own mail** where there is any, and the bell is simply not made; **a line on the person's activity page** (`sv.app.activity.log`) is queued when an import finishes, when an export is ready, and when the person deletes their account from Waymark (that one names the `sub` and no account, since the account is going, is made after they are gone, and is not deleted with them; a deletion that came from the core, which knows, leaves none). Settings > Notifications has **`core` as a channel** (`notice_pref`; on by default for an import) and shows **core's bell switch only where the people here sign in through a core**; email is offered where there is mail of Waymark's or a core to send it. Delivery is told by `noticeDelivery()`. **Seen** against the SDK's `createFakeCore`, on a `file:` database, sqld and PostgreSQL (351 tests in the data-layer files on each; 11 in `src/sovereign/outbox.test.ts`): a notice queues the bell and the email with it and each is made once to the right `sub` with the right link, an email carrying its key; only what the person wants is queued, nothing is queued without a core, and a rolled-back change queues nothing; a person with no `sub` there gets Waymark's mail and no bell; **a 403 sends the email by Waymark's mail, drops the bell, and is rested (a later notice makes no request to core) until the rest is over**; **core down loses nothing, waits the pause (not a millisecond less), and makes each call once, with the same key, when core is back**; core's 429 pause is taken as given; an old call is given up on; **forty calls with two workers running at the same time are each made once**; a line about a deletion survives the removal of the person's own calls and is made; what waits is sealed and a row that cannot be opened is given up on; the three lines of activity are queued by the import, the export and the deletion; **in Chromium (`e2e/sovereign.spec.ts`, the core-only instance):** Settings shows core's bell as a switch, on, and email enabled with no mail of its own, and turning the bell off survives a reload. **Left out:** calls to core are not tried in a browser against a real core (the fake is the SDK's own and the scopes are the manifest's); `sv.as().directory` is 14.2; and an older core that does not list a service is found out on the first call and rested, not read from its instance document in advance. A timeline browser test (editing offline) failed once in a full run under load and passed three times alone; WebKit was not run.

#### ✅ 14.2 — Core's directory

**Goal:** finding people on the instance to invite. **Design:** §11.2, §19.3. **Depends on:** 2.3, 10.2.

**Technical:** `sv.as({ sub }).directory.searchUsers` with the person's refresh token, kept through `src/crypto/` in the SDK's `tokenStore` (how
better-auth's `genericOAuth` feeds it is checked here, or settled by Tally's 11.2 if built first; a gap raised in `sovereign`); results in the invite field
beside Waymark's own; an invitation to a directory person delivered by core's bell and taken by their first sign-in through core by `sub`; scopes refused
means inviting works as alone.

**Accepts when:** against the fake core, a directory person is invited and, signing in through core, lands in the trip with their role.

**Done as:** **The refresh token** (settled here, DESIGN.md §19.3, §27): better-auth's `genericOAuth` does store it for `offline_access`, encrypted with the sign-in secret on the `account` row. The `hooks.after` of `src/auth/auth.ts` (on the callback of the Sovereign provider) hands it, with the account and the `sub`, to `onSovereignSignIn`, which **moves** it into **`core_token`** (migration `0029_coredirectory`; sealed, `SEALED.core_token`, so key rotation reaches it, and in the people-deletion test's lists) and **clears the `account` row's copy**, so a credential is in one place and read only through `src/crypto/`; a failure there is logged by kind and does not undo the sign-in. **`src/sovereign/tokens.ts`** is the SDK's `TokenStore` over it: `write` is a conditional update on the hash of the token read (core takes one used twice for theft), `lock` is a lease in the row (30 s, taken over when it has run out, let go only by its holder), `delete` for core's account-deleted notice, and `deleteCoreTokenOf` in `People.deletePerson`. **`src/sovereign/directory.ts`** is the directory as the person asking (`sv.as({ sub })`): `search` and `resolve`, none of which throws: `found`, `unavailable` (no token, a session core has ended, an older core, the scope refused) and `failed` (down, busy); a core that does not serve it or refuses the scope is rested for six hours, as core's other services are; names are made one line of text. **One SDK client per process** now serves the bell and the directory (`server-core.ts`). **Inviting:** `invite.create` takes a `sub` beside `accountId` and `email`; the server **looks the person up again as the Organizer** (the Organizer's role first, so nothing is asked of core for anyone else) and keeps core's name (sealed) and the `sub` (`invitation.target_sub`); **a person who has signed in here is told by a notice, as any account is, and accepts it; one who has not is told by core's bell** (`core_outbox`, addressed by `sub`, with the link), **and their first sign-in through core takes it** (`claimInvitations`, by the `sub` core vouched for in that sign-in, never by an email: the same statement as accepting, once, a full trip stays waiting, the inviter told). **`people.directory`** (an Organizer's op) searches, leaves out people already in the trip and says nothing of who has an account; limited to a hundred a minute. **The manifest asks for `offline_access` and `directory`** (`SOVEREIGN_SCOPES`, held equal to the manifest by a test); **the invite dialog** offers the instance's people under the Organizer's own after a pause in typing. **`startFakeSovereign`** now runs the SDK's `createFakeCore` behind it (refresh tokens for `offline_access`, the refresh and app-token grants, `/api/v1/*`, the documents at the root, and `core.addPerson` through the e2e control port), so one fake serves the sign-in and the directory. **Seen:** the store (9 tests on the three databases: sealed on disk, conditional write, lease, two callers at once with the SDK's own fake core, which ends a person's tokens when one is used twice, and none was), the directory (8: every way it can be unavailable), invitations (12 more: looked up again by the server, a notice for an account and core's bell for none, taken at sign-in by the sub and by nothing else, a full trip, an account's own invitation not taken at sign-in, the search), the sign-in (2: the token handed over and the copy cleared, and a failing hook does not undo a sign-in), the people-deletion and key-rotation lists, and, in Chromium and WebKit, a person who has never used Waymark invited by name from the directory and put in the trip with the role at their first sign-in through core, the page asking nobody but this server. The mutation checks: a write that ignores the token read, a lease that is always free, a claim by any sub, a name taken from the browser, a copy not cleared and a hook that never matches each fail a test. **Left out:** an invitation for someone who signs in a second time **after** the first (they are taken at the first); the scopes need granting in the instance's Console, which `docs/` for operators says with hosting (16.5); a refresh token unused for 30 days has run out at core, so the person is offered no directory until they sign in again; the core floor stays `>=0.57.0` (the SDK treats an older core as serving the directory).
