# Epic 13: Trip Mode

## Status

📋 Planned.

## Overview

Phase 3: today on an ongoing trip. Today's plan worked out as a pure function before any screen; the screen, phone first; Directions handed off to the
device's maps; reminders through the bell, Web Push and email; and the day's plan and map kept for offline. The plan is shown exactly as ordered; reordering
by distance is later (`DESIGN.md` §26.1).

## Related

[`DESIGN.md`](../../DESIGN.md) §14, §17.3, §8.2

## Tasks

#### ✅ 13.1 — Today

**Goal:** what today looks like, and what is next. **Design:** §14.1. **Depends on:** 9.3, 4.4.

**Technical:** `src/today/`, pure, from a trip's rows, the person's done marks and the moment: today in the current stop's zone, the day or the travel
day's two, items in plan order with timed ones by time, next, the countdown, the empty day; `item_done` as a mutation in the trip's stream.

**Accepts when:** fixtures hold across the date line, at 23:59 and 00:01 local, on a travel day, and with the device in another zone than the stop.

**Done as:** **`src/today/today.ts`**, pure and the one implementation (no clock, no database, no device: `now` and `deviceZone` are given; `todayOfTrip` adapts a trip as `LocalStore.tripView` holds it). **Today's date is the date in the current stop's zone**, never the device's (a phone still set to Tokyo, in Lisbon, is on Lisbon's date; a stop whose place has no zone falls back to the device's). The current stop is the one whose dates contain the date **as its own zone says it is**; on a travel day both stops qualify, the later is the current one, and **both days are shown, departing then arriving**; once the zones disagree (Tokyo has gone on to the 6th while Honolulu is still the 5th) only the stop whose own date it still is remains. A trip with no dates is `planning`; before its first day (by the first stop's zone) `before`, with the date; after its last day (by the last stop's zone) `after`, with the date; on none of its stops' days `between`; otherwise `during`. **Items are in plan order, and the timed ones in time order in the places the timed ones had** (an untimed item stays where the plan put it; ties by position, then id). **Next** is the first item in that order that is not done and is either timed with a start still ahead (the moment is the day's date and the start time **in the day's stop's zone**, across a change of clocks too: `momentOf`) or untimed and after the last item done; a timed item that has passed unmarked is not next and stays in the list undone; the countdown is whole minutes rounded up, none for an untimed one; **a day with nothing in it is an empty day**, not an error. **`item_done`**: a person's own _Done_ mark, a new table (migration `0025_itemdone`: trip, item, account, time; nothing sealed) and a **mutation in the trip's stream** (`{ table: 'item_done', op: 'put' | 'delete', id: <item> }`, in `trips/sync-format.ts` with the rest, checked by the same rules on both sides; `markDone` in `actions/items.ts`). The server applies it in the same one transaction as the other trip changes: by an Organizer or a Traveler **now** (a Follower is refused with the role sentence, a stranger as not in the trip), for **an item of this trip** (an unknown item, or another trip's, is "That item was removed from the trip", kept in Couldn't sync), once (marking twice is marking once), and **a delete takes away only the sender's own mark**. A trip's pull carries **only the account's own marks** (`sets.done`, of items still there: a deleted item's mark is out of sight), so another member never sees them; the device lays the outbox over them (`overlayTrip`), so **a mark made offline moves "next" at once**, reaches the person's other devices, and is taken away by a delete. Marks go with the trip, with the person when they leave it, and with their account (`TRIP_TABLES`, `endMembership`, `endMembershipsOf`, the deletion test). **Seen**, on a `file:` database, sqld and PostgreSQL (the whole suite, 1570 tests; 21 of them new) and under three machine zones (`pnpm test:zones`, now with the Today fixtures): the day's date from the stop's zone with the device in another; **23:59 and 00:01 local**, whatever UTC says; a change of clocks; a travel day in one zone and **across the date line** (both days while both zones are on the 5th; one once they disagree); before, after, between, planning; the order of a day (including with the rows given in any order); next after the last done, an item done before its time never offered, an item that has passed unmarked skipped, no next at the end, an empty day; the countdown rounded up; the marks' own rules on the server (own, twice, delete only one's own, role, stranger, unknown and other trips' items, a deleted item, trip, leaving, account), and on a device Today from the rows it holds with a mark made offline moving next, reaching the person's second device and not another member's. With each of these taken out one test fails: the stop's zone for the date, the later stop being current, the "ahead" test for a timed item, skipping done items, a delete of anyone's mark, and the item check. **Left out:** the Trip Mode screen and its map (13.2), **checking in from it linking and marking the item done in the journal** (the journal's own "done" is §13's, with 11.5; this is the person's own press), and Directions (13.3); reminders read the same rules (13.4); marks are not in the export.

#### ✅ 13.2 — The Trip Mode screen

**Goal:** `/t/<id>/today`. **Design:** §14.2. **Depends on:** 13.1, 11.4, 7.2.

**Technical:** for Travelers and going Organizers on an Ongoing trip, otherwise when it starts or that it ended; the map with the day's items numbered and
the person's position; next with the countdown, _Directions_ and _Check in_ (pre-filled with the next item's place, copied into the person's places by
4.1's rule, and the trip); the day's items with _Done_; Home's Trip Mode card (7.6) and _Open Trip Mode_ on the trip (11.2).

**Accepts when:** on a mocked clock and position, the screen shows the right day and next item, and checking in from it links to the trip and marks the item
done in the journal.

**Done as:** **`/t/<id>/today` is Trip Mode** (`TripMode` in `TripModeScreen.tsx`; `src/screens/tripmode.ts`, pure, 9 tests), **drawn from the device**, phone first and the same screen wider, and a screen of the offline shell too (`/t/<id>/today` in `src/sw/addresses.ts`). **For whom:** a Traveler, or an Organizer who is going, on a trip that is on (`screenOf`); **otherwise it says why, with _Open the plan_**: a Follower or an Organizer who is not going ("Trip Mode is for the people going", and nothing of the dates), a trip that is not ready ("needs a trip that is ready"), a trip that has not begun ("The trip starts on 1 July 2026") or has ended, and a day between two stops. **What it shows** is `src/today/`'s answer (13.1), for the device's clock and zone: **"Kyoto · day 2"** (or "Lisbon → Sintra · day 3" on a travel day, which shows both days' items under their stops), **a map of the day's items numbered in the order shown and the person's own position** (the map of 11.4 with its `position` dot; only where the server has a map), **Next** with the item, its countdown ("in 40 min", "in 4 h", "in 2 h 5 min") and _Directions_ and _Check in_, and **the day's items in plan order with _Done_** on each (✓ done, ● fixed, ○ to do). **Done is the journal's and the person's own mark**: a check-in at the item's place that day makes it done and it says "Checked in"; _Done_ marks it without a check-in (`markDone`, kept on the device and synced) and _Undo_ takes the mark away. **_Directions_** is `src/directions/` (13.3): the device's own maps, decided from what the browser says of itself once it is in the browser, at the item's point and name; none for an item with no place. **_Check in_ opens the sheet filled in with the item's place and the trip:** `?checkin=new&t=<trip>&item=<item>` (both ids or neither: tested with the odd and hostile cases; closing the sheet takes them out), the place **first, chosen and marked "from the plan"** (made with the same-place rule of §7.1, so a place the person already has is reused, not copied again), and **the trip is the item's** (the device's link hook is told). The item is then done in the journal and the next one is next. **Home's Trip Mode card and _Open Trip Mode_ on a trip** (7.6, 11.2) go here. **Seen, on a mocked clock and position (`e2e/tripmode.spec.ts`, in Chromium and WebKit):** at ten on the second day of a Lisbon trip the heading is "Lisbon · day 2"; the map has the three items numbered 1 to 3 and the person's position; **Next is "Tram 28, in 4 h"** (Breakfast at 09:30 had passed and was not done, so it is not next), _Directions_ is an OpenStreetMap link (a desktop browser's); _Done_ on Breakfast makes it done and _Undo_ undoes it; _Check in_ from Next opens the sheet with "Elétrico …" chosen and "from the plan"; after checking in **the Tram is done ("Checked in"), Dinner is next "in 9 h" and the check-in the server has is of the trip**; and a trip that starts in July, one that ended in May and one still planning each say so with a way to the plan, and a trip that is not there says so. The rules broken in turn (anyone going; the countdown's hours) made their tests fail. **Left out:** a countdown that ticks by the second (the screen reads its clock every 30 seconds); the day's items cannot be reordered (§26.1); no push or reminder is raised from here (13.4); the kept tiles for the map with no network (13.5).

#### ✅ 13.3 — Directions

**Goal:** a hand-off to the device's maps. **Design:** §14.3. **Depends on:** 13.2.

**Technical:** `geo:` on Android, `maps://` on iPhone and iPad, an OpenStreetMap directions link elsewhere, decided from the device; the item's point and
name; documented in the README.

**Accepts when:** on the iOS Simulator, Directions opens Apple Maps at the item, not a browser tab.

**Done with 13.2:** the button is _Directions_ on Trip Mode's Next card (`TripModeScreen.tsx`), a link made by `directionsLink` for the platform `platformOf` decides from `userAgent`, `platform` and `maxTouchPoints`; the README's "Directions" section says the convention. **Not seen:** the acceptance names the iOS Simulator, and the link on an iPhone is `maps://?daddr=…`, which the unit tests hold exactly and the browser test sees as OpenStreetMap on a desktop browser; whether Safari on a simulator opens Apple Maps at the item has not been tried here.

**Done so far (the hand-off; the button waits for 13.2's screen):** **`src/directions/directions.ts`**, pure: `platformOf` decides the device from what a browser says (`userAgent`, `platform`, `maxTouchPoints`: Android; iPhone, iPad and iPod; **an iPad that says it is a Mac, told apart by its touch screen**; anything else) and `directionsLink` makes the link: **`geo:<lat>,<lng>?q=<lat>,<lng>(<name>)` on Android, `maps://?daddr=<lat>,<lng>&q=<name>` on Apple devices, `https://www.openstreetmap.org/directions?route=;<lat>,<lng>` elsewhere**, with the point kept to five decimals (a metre or so) and **no link for a point that is not one** (out of range, not finite, not a number). A name is one line of at most a hundred characters, without control characters or the parentheses a `geo:` label ends at, and percent-encoded, so that **a place's name cannot add a parameter, a scheme or a line to the link** (tested with a hostile name for each platform). **Documented in the README** ("Directions"): the convention, that nothing is fetched and no position is sent. **Seen** (7 tests): the device for each kind of agent, the three links, the rounding, the refused points, the hostile name, a name in Japanese with parentheses and an ampersand. **Left out, and why the task is not done:** the _Directions_ button on the Trip Mode screen (13.2), which uses these two functions with `navigator`, and a check in a browser that Android and iPhone open their maps apps from the links (no device here).

#### ✅ 13.4 — Reminders

**Goal:** "Dinner, Gion in 30 minutes". **Design:** §14.4. **Depends on:** 13.1, 12.4.

**Technical:** a one-minute job for Ongoing trips and the eve of Upcoming ones: `trip_starts`, `item_soon`, `day_plan`, with defaults; per person and trip
lead time and kinds in the trip's settings; once per item and person (`reminder_sent`), again if the item's time changes; none for untimed items; through
the bell, Web Push, email if chosen, and core's bell when hosted.

**Accepts when:** on a mocked clock, a fixed item's reminder is sent once with two workers, a re-timed item reminds again, and an untimed item never does.

**Done as:** **The rules are pure** (`src/reminders/due.ts`, built on Today's): from a trip's rows, one member's settings and Done marks and the moment, they say what is due. **`trip_starts`** from 18:00 the day before the first day, in the first stop's zone, until it begins; **`item_soon`** a lead time before a timed item on a trip day (30 minutes unless the member chose, 5 to 240), **for fixed items, and for flexible ones only if the member asked, never for an untimed item, never for one the member has marked done**; **`day_plan`** from 08:00 to 20:00 on a trip day, in the day's zone, for a member who asked, in one line, and not for a day with nothing in it. Each has a **key** that names what it is about, and for an item **the time it was at**, so an item moved to a new time can remind again and one moved away from now does not. **`reminder_pref`** and **`reminder_sent`** (migration `0027_reminders`): a member's settings **for a trip** (`trip_starts` and `item_soon` on, 30 minutes, flexible off, `day_plan` off, when they have changed none) read and changed by them alone through the trip's operations `reminder.get` and `reminder.set` (Organizers and Travelers; for a Follower, a former member, a stranger and a trip that is not there, not found; a value that is not one is refused in a sentence that repeats nothing); and the record of what was sent. **The job `reminders`** (every minute, under a lease as well) looks at the ready trips within two days of now, and for each member who goes (an Organizer who goes, or a Traveler) asks the pure rules; **each reminder is sent once: its key is written in the same transaction as the notice, and the primary key decides which of two workers sends it** (a worker that dies between the two sends none half). A reminder is a notice (`trip_starts`, `item_soon`, `day_plan`, new kinds with defaults: the bell always, email for a trip's start, core's bell, and **push on for all three**, which applies only to a person who turned push on, 12.4), so it reaches the person by the channels they chose kind by kind, and opens `/t/<id>/today`; the words name the item and the time (they are sealed in the bell and in the person's own mail) and the push carries only the kind and the path. The record is kept a month; the settings and the record go with the trip, with the person when they leave, and with their account. **Seen**, on a `file:` database, sqld and PostgreSQL (the whole suite, 1623 tests; 29 of them new): to the minute (17:59 no, 18:00 yes; 18:29 no, 18:30 yes for a fixed item; nothing at its time), in the stop's zone with the phone set to another; flexible only when asked; untimed never; done never; the lead time and its bounds; a moved item reminds again at its new time and not at its old; the plan of the day from 08:00 to 20:00, once a day, not for an empty day; **sent once to the three members who go and not to the Follower, a trip still being planned, or an Organizer who does not go; once with two workers at the same moment**; the member's own settings (one with a lead of ten minutes and flexible items, one with item reminders off); the plan of the day only for who asked; a push queued for a person who has a device and is not for one who turned the kind off; the operations as above; gone with the leaving person and the deleted trip; forgotten after a month. With each of these taken out one test fails: the key (a reminder per minute), `going`, and `ready`. **One guard is defence in depth**: the role filter (a Follower is never "going", the role change sets it). **Left out:** the screen for the per-trip settings (the trip's page, 11.x; the operations exist); **a reminder that opens `/t/<id>/today` before 13.2 builds that page** (the link is the design's, and the notice and the push say the right thing); reminders for the eve of a trip are sent from 18:00 even if the trip was made ready later that evening (the first minute after); and a reminder missed because the server was down for the whole of its window is not sent late (a reminder after the time is no reminder).

#### ✅ 13.5 — A trip offline

**Goal:** the day's plan and map with no network. **Design:** §14.5. **Depends on:** 13.2, 6.6, 1.11.

**Technical:** the worker keeps tiles around each stop of an Upcoming or Ongoing trip, to a street level and a bounded count per trip, fetched through
`/api/map`; _Download for offline_ on the trip; removed after the trip ends or at sign-out; Trip Mode and the trip's plan answered offline by 6.4's shell;
Settings > This device shows kept tiles and their size.

**Accepts when:** with the network off after downloading, Trip Mode shows the day with its map around the stop, and the kept tiles stay within the bound.

**Done as:** **_Download for offline_** is on a trip that is upcoming or on (`OfflineTrip`, in the Overview): it keeps **the map's files around every stop, to a street level, and a bounded number for the trip** (`src/map/tilemath.ts`, pure, 7 tests, the tiles checked against values worked out apart from this code): the style for each theme and the two fonts' Latin glyphs, then the tiles around each stop at zooms 3 to 14 (one tile at the coarse zooms, the three by three around it from 8 to 13, the five by five at 14, which is where the style's tiles stop), **coarse zooms first for every stop**, so a bound of 600 tiles cuts the finest tiles of the last stops and never a stop's whole map, and every address once. **The worker keeps them** (`src/sw/source.ts`, 7 new tests): the page names the files by a message and the worker fetches them, six at a time, **into a cache of its own** (`waymark-tiles-v1`, which the shell's clean-up does not touch), reports progress, and answers the map's requests for them from it, **with no network too**; **only the map's own public files are taken, whatever is asked**: a path of the style's tiles, the style for `light` or `dark`, or a font's glyph range, of this origin; any other address, another origin's, a path that only looks like one, and anything that is not a string are left out (tested with the hostile ones, and the shell's allow-list is unchanged: nothing of anyone's is in either cache), at most a thousand addresses in a message and six thousand files in all; a file that was not named is not kept just because the map asked for it, and with no network and not kept it is a gap (a 503), not an error page. **Let go of** by `src/map/offline.ts` (9 tests): a record of each trip's files and last day, on the device and one for each account, so **a file is dropped only when no trip still wanted needs it** (two stops close together, or two trips to one city, share tiles); a trip's maps are let go **the day after it ends, when the trip is gone from the device, when the person removes them** (_Remove from this device_), **at sign-out** (the record and the worker's files) **and from Settings** (_Remove the kept maps_). **Settings, This device** shows **how many map files are kept and how big they are**. Trip Mode and the trip's map open where the day is (not on the whole world, which would ask for tiles nothing could have kept), and `/t/<id>/today` is a screen of the offline shell, so the day with its map draws with no network. **Seen (`e2e/offline-trip.spec.ts`, in Chromium and WebKit):** on an ongoing trip _Download for offline_ keeps the map ("The map around the stops is kept on this device"); the worker's cache holds the style, the glyphs and the tile around the stop, **only the map's own files, within the bound**; Settings says "N map files, …"; **with the network off Trip Mode draws, with the map and the day's numbered item, and every tile the map asked for came from the worker's cache (none a gap)**; and _Remove from this device_ empties the worker's cache and offers the download again. Each rule was broken in turn and seen to fail (coarse zooms first, the worker keeping any address, a shared file dropped with a trip). **Left out:** the tiles are fetched in the page's session at the time of the download (there is no background refresh when a trip's stops change: _Download again_ does it); a push or a reminder with no network (push needs one, §14.5); the glyph ranges beyond the Latin letters (labels in other scripts show their Latin name offline, as the map's design says); a size bound in bytes (the count is the bound).
