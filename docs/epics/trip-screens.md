# Epic 11: Trip screens

## Status

✅ Done: 11.1 to 11.8, run in Chromium and WebKit.

## Overview

Phase 2's interface: the trips list and the trip page the plugin deferred, designed now, with its views (Overview, Plan, Map, Journal, Files, People). The
plugin's Planner is the trip's Plan view. Everything paints from the device's trip streams (6.6).

## Related

[`DESIGN.md`](../../DESIGN.md) §10, §11, §13, §20.9, §20.10

**Goal:** `/trips` and the sidebar's Trips. **Design:** §20.9, §20.2. **Depends on:** 9.4, 6.6, 7.1.

**Technical:** status chips and search; groups by status in §20.9's order; cards with dot, name, dates, days, stops in order, status, initials, and the
action by status; _New trip_ dialog (name, colour, optionally the first stop); the sidebar's Trips section shown (ongoing, upcoming, planning, the latest
three completed); the timeline's trip filter and dot.

**Accepts when:** a seeded trip of each status shows in the right group with the right action, and a new trip appears in the sidebar at once.

**Done as:** **`/trips` is one page drawn from the device** (`TripsView`, `app/(app)/trips/page.tsx`): it reads the trips the store holds (`LocalContext.trips`, new, from `LocalStore.trips()`), so it opens with no network, and is also a screen of the offline shell (`/trips` in `src/sw/addresses.ts`, `OfflineApp`). **`src/screens/trips.ts`, pure and tested:** a card per trip with its **status worked out on the device** by 9.4's `statusOf`, **in the first stop's zone**, with the device's clock (re-read each minute, `useTrips.ts`), because the status the server last said can be a day old (a trip that begins in Auckland begins at Auckland's midnight, tested for Auckland and Lisbon at the same instant); the dates, the days, the stops in order, the members for their initials, and **one action by status and role** (_Open journal_ when completed, _Open Trip Mode_ when ongoing for an Organizer or Traveler who is going, _Continue planning_ when planning for an editor, else _View plan_; a Follower, and a member who is not going, never get Trip Mode); the groups in §20.9's order (ongoing, upcoming by date, planning by latest change, completed newest first, the same whatever order the rows are held in); the chips with their counts; the search over the name and the stops (every word, any order, no case or accents); the sidebar's list (ongoing, upcoming, planning and the latest three completed); and the dot's token for each colour, which a test holds to the design system's own tokens. **The chip, the search and the open dialog are in the address** (`?status=`, `?q=`, `?new=1`), so a link to _New trip_ is a link and a reload keeps them. **_New trip_** (`NewTripDialog`): a name, a colour from the six roles, and optionally the first stop found by the place search; **an online operation** (`trip.create`, then `stop.add`), then `syncNow()`, so the card and the sidebar show it at once; with no connection it says in a sentence that a trip is made on the server, and a first stop that fails leaves the trip made and says so. A place search that is off, not answering or offline says so in the dialog and does not hide the name and colour. **The sidebar's Trips section** (`AppSidebar`, in the drawer too): full, each trip with its dot, name and status, then _All trips_ and _New trip_; **as a rail, each trip is its dot with the name as its label and tooltip**, and one icon for the list. **`/t/<id>` was a placeholder here** until 11.2 built it. **The timeline's trip filter and dot** (`?trip=<id>`, a _Trip_ select once the device holds a trip, a dot with the trip's name on a check-in linked to one; `parseFilters` accepts only a trip id). Both routes are in `src/access/routes.ts`.
**Seen:** 14 unit tests for the model (above) and one for the timeline filter (and the hostile queries); the status in two zones, the action by role, the groups, the chips and the sidebar's three completed each failed when the rule was taken out (the first stop's zone, and "an editor" for Trip Mode, were broken in turn and seen to fail); **in Chromium (`e2e/trips.spec.ts`):** a trip of each status (made through the operations, with dates around today) is in its own group with the right action and address, the groups are in order, a chip narrows to its status and is in the address, a search narrows by name and says when nothing matches, a trip made in the dialog with a first stop is in the list and the sidebar at once, and the sidebar links go to the list and the dialog (full on Home, as dots on any other page). #### ✅ 11.3 — The plan

**Goal:** the Plan view, the plugin's Planner remade. **Design:** §20.10, §10.3–§10.5. **Depends on:** 11.2, 9.3, 17.7.

**Technical:** stops across the top (reorder, re-date with 17.7, _Add a stop_ through the place search, remove); the selected stop's days below, columns
wide and sections narrow, each with title, notes and items; drag within and between days (`@dnd-kit`, with keyboard moves); an item beside the plan or in a
sheet (place or title, kind, times, fixed disabled until a time, notes, its day); shrinking a stop over items opens the move-or-delete dialog; read-only
for Followers; the clock mark on unsynced items.

**Accepts when:** a trip is planned from nothing with the keyboard only; an item edited offline syncs; a Follower can change nothing.

**Done as, except an item beside the plan (it opens as a dialog, full screen on a phone):** **`?view=plan` of the trip's page is the Plan** (`PlanView`): **the stops across the top** as a `StepStrip` of buttons (the selected one in `?stop=`, so a link and a reload keep it; the first by default), **the selected stop's days below**, a column each on a wide screen and a section each on a narrow one (a grid that wraps), each with its date, title, notes and items, and _Add an item_ at the foot. **`src/screens/plan.ts`, pure and tested (11 tests):** a stop's days by date with their items **as Trip Mode shows them** (`displayOrder`); and **where a drop goes**: what a person sees is the plan's order with the timed items in time order in the timed slots, and what is stored is a position, so a drop is turned into the stored place that _shows_ the item where it was dropped by trying each place and asking `displayOrder` (it cannot drift from the screen; the same-day drop takes the neighbour's place as an array move does, a drop on another day goes just before the item it is dropped on or to the end, a drop where it already is does nothing, and a timed item goes where its time says). **Items are written through the one path** (`createItem`, `editItem`, `moveItem`, `deleteItem`, `editDay` and `local.record`, which now takes any mutation): they show at once, work with no network, and carry **a clock until the server has them** (`TripData.pending`, the ids in the outbox for the trip); an edit that moves an item to another day is the edit and then the move, in order. **Moving:** by the **drag handle** (`@dnd-kit`, the design system's `useReorderSensors`: mouse by the handle, touch by a long press, keyboard sensor) within a day and to another day of the stop (an empty day is a place to drop), and by **Move up / Move down buttons** and the **Day** field of the item's dialog, so nothing needs a pointer. **The item's dialog** (`ItemDialog`): title, place (one the trip has, or searched for), kind, start and end time (an end needs a start; taking the start away takes the end and _Fixed_ with it; _Fixed_ is disabled until there is a start), notes and the day; the rules are the server's, run first on the device, and a refusal is a sentence in the dialog. **A new place is the one online part** (`item.add` or `item.update` with the place, then a sync), because the place is made on the server (§7.1); with no connection the search says so and the trip's own places are still there. **Stops are online operations** (`StopDialogs`): _Add a stop_ (a place through the place search and, optionally, dates with the `DateRangePicker`), _Change dates_, _Move earlier_ / _Move later_ (only for a stop with no dates: a stop with dates is in the order of its dates and moves by being re-dated, as the server says), and _Remove the stop_ (asking first, naming the days and items that go with it). **Shrinking a stop over days with items is never silent**: the server refuses naming the dates, and the dialog says which days, and offers to move their items to another day of the trip or delete them, then tries again. A day's title and notes are edited in a small dialog. **A Follower** is shown the same plan and none of its controls (no handle, no buttons, no dialogs mounted).
**Seen:** the 11 unit tests, with the rules each taken out in turn and seen to fail (the drop on a day going to the start, the same-day drop on the neighbour, a drop on itself still moving, the days not in date order); **in Chromium (`e2e/plan.spec.ts`):** **a trip is planned from nothing with the keyboard alone** (an empty trip, _Add a stop_ by Enter, the place typed and chosen, the dates by the calendar's keys, three days appear, two items added by Enter, one moved above the other with the Move up button, the first's Up and the last's Down disabled); **an item edited with the network off** shows at once with its new title and time and the clock, the clock goes when the network is back, and a reload shows the same from the server; **an item dragged by its handle to another day** lands before the item there and leaves its old day empty; **a stop shortened over a day with items** (taking its dates away) is refused naming the day, _Move the items_ is disabled until a day is chosen, the items go to the other stop's day and the first stop has no days; and **a Follower** (rows put on the device with the network off) sees the stop and the item and has none of _Add a stop_, _Add an item_, _Change dates_, _Remove the stop_, _Edit_, _Delete_, _Move_ or _Drag_ (and putting the controls back for a Follower made that test fail). The unit suites passed on a `file:` database, sqld and PostgreSQL; the Chromium suite passed. **Found on the way:** the server names the days that would go **by date**, not by id (the dialog maps them to the stop's days); and the design system's range calendar does not take the focus when it opens, so a keyboard user reaches its grid with Tab (it works from there; worth raising in `sovereign`). **Left out:** an item beside the plan on a wide screen (`SplitPane`); dragging a stop (an undated one moves by the buttons, and a dated one by its dates); dragging an item to a day of another stop (the item's Day field does it); the place of an item cannot be changed to a new one with no network; a stop's note is shown and not edited; WebKit was not run.

#### ✅ 11.5 — Planned versus actual

**Goal:** the Journal view. **Design:** §13. **Depends on:** 11.2, 10.3.

**Technical:** `src/journal/`, pure: done, skipped, found on the way, untimed and placeless items as planned, using 4.1's match rule over the check-ins the
reader may see; each day as a sentence and a list in time order with who checked in and their photos; the trip's figures for Overview.

**Accepts when:** a hand-worked trip gives the expected done, skipped and found counts per day, and a reader never sees an unshared check-in of another.

**Done as:** **`src/journal/`, pure and the one implementation** (`journal.ts`, `trip.ts`, 19 tests): from a trip's stops, days and items, its members, **the reader's own check-ins linked to the trip and the copies others shared with it**, and `now` and the device's zone, it gives each day as a list in time order and a sentence ("5 planned, 3 done, 1 skipped, 2 found on the way"), the trip's figures, and the set of items that are done. **Done**: an item whose place is the same place as a check-in on that day (§7.1: the same name within 50 metres). **Skipped**: an item with a place that nothing matched, once its day has passed **in the zone of its stop**. **Planned**: everything else, including every item with **no place**, which nothing can match and so is never judged. **Found on the way**: a check-in on that day that matches no item, put in the list at its own time (before the first timed item that starts after it). A check-in is on the day it was **by its own clock**; a date two stops share (a travel day) is one day of the journal with the items of both; the journal runs to today, and a day to come is not in it. **Only Travelers and Organizers who are going count** (§11.1): a check-in by an Organizer who is not going, by a Follower or by someone no longer in the trip is left out (tested, and an Organizer who then goes counts). **The reader never sees an unshared check-in of another, because it is never an input**: the adapter (`journalOfTrip`) takes only the reader's own and the copies the server gave the device, and the reader's own that follow another trip or none are left out (tested, and with that check taken out the test fails). **A reading of the design, confirmed by the owner (`DESIGN.md` §13 says it):** "untimed or placeless items show as planned, never judged" is read as **placeless** items only; an untimed item with a place is judged like any other, since the plan's time plays no part in a match and most of what is planned has none; `judgeUntimed: false` gives the other reading, and is tested. **The Journal** (`JournalView`, `?view=journal`): each day's heading, stops and sentence, and its entries with a status (_Done_, _Skipped_, _Planned_, _Found on the way_), the time, who checked in (name and initials) and their photos (`PhotoImage`; the reader's own and those of check-ins shared with the trip, which `/api/media` serves to its members, Followers included, §11.3); a trip that has not begun says there is nothing to look back on yet. **The Overview's figures are the journal's**: _Places planned_, _Places visited_ (a planned place that is done on some day) and _Check-ins_ (the reader's own linked ones and those others shared), and **its next thing agrees with the journal**: an item a check-in made done is not next, as if the person had marked it (Trip Mode, 13.2, takes the journal's `doneItems` with the person's own marks).
**Seen:** the 19 unit tests (a trip worked by hand with a travel day, four people and check-ins of every kind: the counts and the sentence for each day, who and their photos, skipped only once the day is over in the stop's zone, a place matched only by name within 50 m, the order of the rows making no difference, the figures), with the rules each taken out in turn and seen to fail (anyone counts, the day by UTC, skipped before the day is over, a placeless item judged, the adapter taking another trip's check-in); **in Chromium (`e2e/journal.spec.ts`):** a trip of two days whose first day reads "3 planned, 1 done, 1 skipped, 1 found on the way" with its entries in time order (done, skipped, planned, found), an item with no place _Planned_ though its day has passed, a check-in at the castle that is not linked to the trip leaving the castle _Skipped_, the café found on the way, the second day "1 planned, 1 skipped", the Overview's _Places visited_ 1 and _Check-ins_ 2, and a trip that has not begun saying there is nothing to look back on. The unit suites passed on a `file:` database, sqld and PostgreSQL; the Chromium suite passed. **Left out:** a check-in found on the way does not link to anything (another person's copy has no page); the journal is not paged (a trip has at most 365 days); WebKit was not run.

#### ✅ 11.6 — Files and links

**Technical:** attachments by day and kind, upload with `FileDropzone`, open (download, or a PDF inline), rename, move, delete; the view hidden from
Followers; links edited from Overview, with _Expenses in Tally_ offered first.

**Accepts when:** an attachment uploaded by a Traveler opens for an Organizer, and the Files view does not exist for a Follower.

**Done as, except moving or reordering a link:** **`?view=files` of the trip's page is the Files** (`FilesView`): the trip's attachments **by day and kind** (`src/screens/files.ts`, pure, 5 tests: the trip's first, then each day's in the order of the trip, within a group by kind (receipt, booking, accommodation, ticket, other) and then title; a file on a day the device does not hold stays in view under _Other_ and is not lost), each with its thumbnail (an image) or icon, title, kind, type and size, and **Open** (a new tab, `noopener`, at its own `/api/media/<id>` address, where it is served with its type, `nosniff`, a sandboxing policy and no cookie to the page), **Download**, **Edit** (title, kind and day: `attachment.update`) and **Delete** (asking first: `attachment.delete`). **Adding** is the design system's `FileDropzone` (choose or drop; PDF or a picture, up to 15 MB), whose name starts the title, then the kind and the day, and `POST /api/trips/<id>/attachments` (the title in a header, as the route says); the server's sentence for a refusal is shown as it is (a file that is not a PDF or a picture, too big, too many, too many at once), and the answer is pulled into the device at once. **The view does not exist for a Follower** (`viewsFor`, 11.2), who is never given an attachment, a row or a file; for anyone else the server's own checks stand whatever the page shows. **Links are edited from the Overview** (`LinkDialogs`): _Add Expenses in Tally_ is offered first, and not again once the trip has one, then _Add a link_; the kind is chosen with Tally first; an address that is not `https` is refused with the server's sentence; with no title a Tally link is called _Expenses in Tally_; change and remove (asking first) are on each link, for Organizers and Travelers; at ten links it says so. A link is opened by the person's own browser (`noopener noreferrer`) and never fetched. **Client-safe constants:** the attachment kinds and the title limit moved to `src/trips/attachment-format.ts` (re-exported by `attachments.ts`), since a screen may not import the server module that holds the database. **A correction to 11.5:** its Done as said members could not be shown the photos of others' check-ins; `/api/media` does serve a photo of a check-in shared with a trip the reader is in (`readSharedPhoto`), so the Journal now shows them.
