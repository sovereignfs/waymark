# Using Waymark

A short guide for the people who use it. Whoever runs your Waymark sets up the address and the sign-in; this is what you do with it.

## Signing in

Open the address you were given. If your Waymark is part of a Sovereign instance, choose **Sign in with Sovereign**; otherwise sign in with your email and password, or a passkey. The first person to open a new Waymark makes the
first account on the sign-up page. Settings is where you set up a passkey or an authenticator app, change your name, and choose which notices reach you and how.

## Checking in

**Check in** is the button at the top of the sidebar (and the key `c`). Choose where you are:

1. Search for the place by name, or choose one you have been to before. If the place is not found, add it by hand, with a name and a point.
2. Add a note, who was with you, and photos if you like. Nothing is required but the place.
3. Save. The check-in has the moment you made it, in the time zone you were in, even if you save it later.

A check-in is yours alone. It is written on your device first, so **you can check in with no connection**: it is kept on the device and sent when the connection comes back. If the server ever refuses a change, it is kept
and not thrown away.

You can change or delete a check-in from its page. **Check-ins** lists them newest first, with a search that looks at places and notes, and filters by year, category and country. **Places** lists every place you have been to,
the most visited first, with how many times; open one to see its check-ins, rename it, or **merge** two that are the same place.

## Working offline

Waymark installs as an app on your phone or computer (use your browser's _Install_ or _Add to Home Screen_). What you do while you have no connection (checking in, editing, deleting) is kept on your device and sent when you
are back online. Searching for a new place and the map need a connection. Opening Waymark itself with no connection is still being built.

## Bringing your places in

**Settings, Data** has **Import a Waymark file**: choose a ZIP made by **Export** (in the same place), and its places, check-ins and photos are added to your account as new ones, by a background job you can leave
while it works. A check-in that is already here is not added twice, and one you deleted stays deleted. Importing from Swarm (Foursquare) is coming.

**Export** makes one ZIP of everything that is yours, also in the background; you are told when it is ready. Deleting your account (Settings, Data) removes everything of yours, files included, from the server.

## What stays private

Your places, notes, names and photos are encrypted on the server. A check-in reaches another person only if you share it with a trip they are on (when trips arrive), and then as a copy: the people following a trip never
receive your photos. There are no points, badges or streaks, and nobody is ranked.
