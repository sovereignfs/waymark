# Security review: phase 2 (0.2.0)

What phase 2 adds is **other people**: trips with members in three roles, check-ins shared with a trip, invitations, attachments, links, auto-link across
members, trips in export and import, and the screens for them. This is the review of 16.2 repeated for it, in the same form: the rule, **where it is
held** (the test that fails when it stops being true), what was found, and whether it was fixed or recorded. `0.1.0.md` is the baseline and is not
repeated; a finding there that phase 2 changes is named.

**State of the run:** `pnpm test` 1817 passed, 4 skipped (four tests that belong to another database leg); the phase 2 browser journeys
(`e2e/trip*.spec.ts`, `people.spec.ts`, `phone.spec.ts`) passed in Chromium and WebKit when they were written (11.8) and again, for the map ones, in this run.

**Not covered.** Core's directory (14.2: inviting a person by name from core's users) is not built; this review covers an invitation by link and by an
address, and **is amended when 14.2 is done**. Nothing was run against a live instance (16.5).

## 1. Roles in every statement (DESIGN.md §5.2, §10.1)

- **The permission is in the statement.** `src/access/trip.ts` holds one fragment (`memberOf`) and three sets of roles (`ORGANIZERS`, `EDITORS`
  = Organizer and Traveler, `MEMBERS` = all three) so that no statement spells the rule for itself. A trip's rows carry `trip_id`, and every read
  and write of them names the trip **together with the caller's membership and the role the operation needs** in the same query, never checked
  first and then touched. **A refusal reads as not found**: a trip that is not yours is a trip that is not there, and the answer is the one for a
  trip that never was.
- **Each operation is called as each lower role.** `src/trips/*.test.ts` and `src/sharing/sharing.test.ts` call each operation as a Traveler, a
  Follower, a former member and a stranger, and assert the same not-found answer and that nothing changed (for example `leaving.test.ts` "is refused
  to a Traveler, a Follower, a stranger and a trip that is not there, all as not found"; `attachments.test.ts` "is for an Organizer or a Traveler");
  `src/trips/route.test.ts` holds that **a request that names an account is not an operation** (the account is the session's alone).
- **Roles that change in the middle of a request.** A caller lowered to Follower while a file was being stored has the file removed
  (`attachments.test.ts`); two Organizers leaving at the same moment never leave the trip with none (`leaving.test.ts`); two accepting one
  invitation at the same moment is won by one (`invitations.test.ts`).
- **Finding 1.1 (recorded).** The three databases do not give a transaction the same isolation (SQLite serialises writers; PostgreSQL's default is
  read committed). The places where two callers at once could break a rule are tested with two callers at the same moment, on the leg the run is on
  (the last Organizer leaving, an invitation accepted twice); **a change to the data layer is run on all three legs** (`AGENTS.md`), which is where
  a difference would show. This review ran the file leg; the sqld and PostgreSQL legs are run again before 0.2.0 is published.

## 2. The device and the stream (DESIGN.md §14, §15)

- **A write goes through one path.** An action in `src/actions/` makes the mutation; `src/sync/apply.ts` applies it, **re-checking ownership or
  role** in one short transaction that increments the stream's `seq`; a replay writes nothing (`apply.test.ts` "is once: a replay writes nothing"),
  and a trip's mutations are whole or not at all: a refused change keeps none of the others' (`src/sync/trips.test.ts`). **Nothing anyone typed is
  thrown away:** a refused change goes to Couldn't sync, with the sentence.
- **A person who has left, was lowered or was removed is refused with a sentence** and nothing is kept (`sync/trips.test.ts` "are refused for a
  Follower, a person who has left and a stranger"); the device then forgets the trip and every row of it (`local/trips.test.ts` "forgets a trip, and
  every row of it, when the account is not in it any more"); the pull never shows one person another's rows (`sync/endpoint.test.ts`).
- **Finding 2.1 (recorded).** Between the moment a person is removed and the moment their device next syncs, the device still holds what it had
  already pulled of that trip, as it would after any copy; **nothing new reaches it**, since the pull is under the same statements. This is the
  cost of working with no network (§14). The user guide does not say so yet; it is written with the documents (16.3).

## 3. Sharing a check-in with a trip (DESIGN.md §11.3)

- **A check-in is its author's.** It reaches another person only as **a copy in the trip's stream**, written when the author shares it and removed
  with it. `sharing.test.ts` holds: private to its author whatever the trip, with no copy existing; given to every member once shared, Followers
  too, **with its place, moment, note, companions and photos and nothing of the author**; not given to a stranger, a former member or for a trip that
  is not there (all the same answer); removed at once when unshared, unlinked by hand, linked to another trip or deleted, each taking a number
  in the trip's stream; **never two copies of one check-in, and another account's mutation cannot replace it**.
- **Leaving removes every copy of the person's** from the trip and keeps the check-ins theirs (`sharing.test.ts`, `leaving.test.ts`); a Traveler
  made a Follower has nothing shared any more and must share again; deleting the trip removes the copies and leaves the originals unlinked.
- **Photos of a shared check-in** are served to every member and to nobody else, **from the trip the reader is in**: a shared check-in of another
  trip they are in does not open this trip's photos (`sharing.test.ts`, "are not served from a trip the reader is not in").
- **Notices about sharing** name the trip and nothing of the check-in or its author, and only to members who chose to hear of them.
- **Finding 3.1 (recorded).** A shared check-in's **place name and note are the author's words**, shown to every member including Followers. They
  are rendered as text (`0.1.0.md` §5) and sealed in the trip's copy as in the original. The author is asked once, when the trip has other members,
  before the first share (`rules.test.ts`); the default is to share nothing.

## 4. Invitations (DESIGN.md §11.2, §19.1)

- **The token is 32 random bytes, shown once in the link, stored only as its hash (SHA-256), compared in constant time** (`token.ts`;
  `invitations.test.ts` "is 32 random bytes, hashed for keeping…"); a string of another shape is not looked up at all. It lasts 14 days, is used
  once, and a link that has been revoked, used, run out or never was is **the same page and the same 404**, never carrying the token back
  (`invitations.test.ts`, "shows a link to anyone and the same 404 for every link that cannot be used"). What the link shows to whoever holds it is
  the trip's name and dates, who invited and the role, and nothing else.
- **Sending is an Organizer's alone**, limited each hour for each person; "which account has this address" is limited as well, so that it cannot be
  used to list who has an account; the answer is the same for an address that has an account and one that has not (`invitations.test.ts` "is by
  an address: a notice to an account that has it, an email to any other, and the answer says the same of both"). **At most fifty are waiting** in a
  trip; a resend makes a new link and ends the old one; what was used, revoked or run out a month ago is forgotten.
- **Accepting is for the signed-in account**, once, with a body that is exactly a token; a full trip is refused in words and the invitation is not
  used up. **An invitation is never matched to an account by its email at sign-in**; accepting is the account's own act.
- **Sign-up by invitation** (`WAYMARK_AUTH_SIGNUP=invite`, the default): the token travels in the `X-Waymark-Invite` header, one hook decides for
  every way an account is made, and a token that does not open an invitation is the same refusal as none (`0.1.0.md` §6).
- **Finding 4.1 (recorded; carried from `0.1.0.md` 6.1).** An invitation link **that has not been accepted yet can be used to make more than one
  account**, since the invitation is spent when it is accepted into the trip and not when an account is made. The link goes to its holder only, the
  sign-up route is rate limited, and an account made this way holds nothing until it is in the trip; the cost of the alternative (spending the link
  at sign-up) is that a sign-up that fails halfway burns it. **Accepted as it is**; a design decision, not a defect to fix quietly.
- **Finding 4.2 (recorded).** A link is a bearer credential, as in every app that has them: whoever holds it before the person it was meant for can
  accept it. The Organizer sees who has joined and can remove them (§10.4); the link can be revoked at once.

## 5. Attachments and files of a trip (DESIGN.md §10.6, §18.3)

- **Followers never receive an attachment** (`ATTACHMENT_READERS = EDITORS`): not a row, not a file, not a thumbnail, not a count. Held at the
  three places a Follower could learn of one: the list ("says nothing of them to a Follower, a former member or a stranger"), the file ("a
  Follower gets not found for the file, and for the thumbnail") and **the stream** (`sync/trips.test.ts` "holds no attachment for a Follower: not
  a set, and not a deletion"), so even the device's store of a Follower never held one.
- **A trip's file has no account:** it is not found by a person's own file route, not even by the person who added it, and not counted against
  anyone's room; it is served by its id **from its own trip only** (`attachments.test.ts`).
- **Uploads** are the store's (`0.1.0.md` §3): judged by bytes (an HTML page named `.pdf` is refused), images re-encoded with the metadata removed,
  everything sealed, **the title sealed and never in the address** (it travels in a header; `attachments.test.ts`), a limit per trip and a size cap.
- **Removing a person removes nothing of the trip's:** what they planned and attached is the trip's (`leaving.test.ts`), and **their photos stop
  being served to the trip** (their shared check-ins' copies go).
- **Finding 5.1 (recorded).** An attachment of a **former member who was an Organizer or a Traveler stays with the trip** (the booking is the
  trip's), and so does the file they added: a person who wants their file gone from a trip must ask an Organizer to delete it. Not in the user guide
  yet (16.3); the alternative (deleting a person's contributions when they leave) would break a plan other people are following.
- **Finding 5.2 (recorded).** The orphan sweep covers a trip's files too (`attachments.test.ts` "leaves files with no row for the sweep… and the
  sweep takes them"): a crash between a row's deletion and its file's leaves a sealed file nothing names, for a short while.

## 6. Auto-link across members (DESIGN.md §12)

- **A check-in is linked only to a trip its author goes on**: never a Follower's, a stranger's, an Organizer's who is not going, or a former
  member's (`autolink.test.ts` "is never linked to a trip the person does not go on…").
- **A link by hand is never changed by anything automatic**: a re-date of the trip or a stop moves what the job linked and keeps the person's
  own (`autolink.test.ts` "keeps one by hand through the re-date"); **another person's check-ins are not read** by the job for someone else's trip
  (`src/autolink/server.ts` reads the account's own memberships and places by statements that name the account).
- **Finding 6.1 (recorded).** When a person joins a trip, **their check-ins that fall in its dates are offered to be linked**, not linked, and
  never shared without their answer (`invitations.test.ts` "their check-ins that fall in the dates are asked to be linked"). The offer names the
  trip, which they already know since they accepted.

## 7. Trips in export, import and deletion (15.3, DESIGN.md §6.4)

- **Export is the person's own**: `export.test.ts` "holds nothing of anyone else, and no address: not the person's own, not another's, not Grace's
  places, notes or photo"; trips in export and import are `trips.test.ts`. An email is shown only to whom §6.1 allows, through the one visibility
  function (`src/access/visibility.ts`, `visibility.test.ts`).
- **Import** is refused whole, with a sentence, for anything that is not a Waymark file (`import.test.ts`), and what it makes is the importer's own.
- **Deleting a person** (`People.deletePerson`) handles every table phase 2 added: `people.test.ts` seeds a row in every table for the person and for a
  second person, finds nothing of the first after and the second's intact, and **fails for a table that is in none of its lists**. A trip they were
  the only Organizer of is handed on, and an empty one deleted.
- **Finding 7.1 (recorded).** Deleting an account that is a trip's **only Organizer** does not refuse: `endMembershipsOf` hands the trip on (and tells
  the member it passes to, `organizer_passed`) or deletes an empty one. Leaving, by contrast, asks the person to name another first (`leaving.test.ts`
  "must name another first"). The difference is deliberate: a person deleting their account cannot be made to wait on a choice. **What the person
  planned and attached stays with the trip**, as in §5.

## Result

No defect found in phase 2's rules that needed a code change; findings recorded (1.1, 2.1, 3.1, 4.1 carried from phase 1, 4.2, 5.1, 5.2, 6.1, 7.1) with the cost each is accepted
at. **Before 0.2.0 is published:** amend this review for core's directory (14.2), run the update on a live instance with a backup first (it has
migrations; 16.5), and re-run the browser journeys on the published image.
